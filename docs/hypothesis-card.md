# Hypothesis Card

## Observation

In the primary model, some placental genes, including FLT1, show higher RNA expression in early-onset preeclampsia than in spontaneous-preterm controls. These groups overlap in delivery week but are not individually matched.

## Hypothesis

At least some placental expression differences, including higher FLT1 RNA, will replicate in an independent early-onset preeclampsia cohort with well-characterized preterm controls.

## Alternatives and limitations

Differences could reflect delivery week, labour or delivery mode, fetal growth restriction, other clinical factors, or differences in cell mixture. Tissue was collected at delivery, and RNA abundance does not directly measure protein secretion or establish cause.

## Falsifiable follow-up

In an independent cohort, compare PE with appropriately characterized preterm controls while accounting for gestational age and delivery circumstances. Measure FLT1 RNA and the protein product separately. Failure to reproduce the RNA difference under those comparisons would weaken the hypothesis. Cell-type-resolved measurements could test whether the signal is present within a particular cell type.

## Confidence

Exploratory: five primary controls and potential residual confounding limit generalization. Browser observations are qualitative and require independent verification.
